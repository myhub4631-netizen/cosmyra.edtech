Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 3, 2026  |  Last Updated: September 3, 2026

Cosmyra Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Cosmyra", "we", "our", or "us") operates Cosmyra NEET JEE / ExamPrep, an educational platform designed to help students prepare for NEET, JEE and other competitive examinations through practice questions, tests, previous-year questions, performance analytics, study tools and related educational services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect and disclose information when you use our website, mobile application and related services.

By using Cosmyra NEET JEE, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Google User Data Policy Compliance Summary

Cosmyra adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. When you choose Sign in with Google, we access only basic profile data (name, email, profile photo) strictly for authentication and account maintenance. We never sell Google user data, nor do we use it for advertising, lending, or profiling.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

A. Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password or authentication credentials
  • Profile picture, if provided
  • Phone number, if provided
  • Date of birth or age, if required for our services
  • Class, examination preference and educational information
  • Other information you voluntarily provide in your profile

B. Information Received Through Google Sign-In

If you choose "Sign in with Google", we use Google's authentication service to authenticate your account.

Depending on the permissions granted and the Google authentication configuration, we may receive basic account information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Google profile picture
  • Google account identifier associated with the authentication

We use this information to:

  • Create or identify your Cosmyra account
  • Authenticate you securely
  • Maintain your user profile
  • Provide access to your account and educational services
  • Associate your learning activity with your account

We do not receive or store your Google Account password.

We do not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, contacts or other Google services unless such access is explicitly requested and separately disclosed.

Google states that Sign in with Google can provide an app with basic profile information such as name, email address and profile picture after user permission.

2. Educational and Activity Information

When you use our platform, we may collect information about your learning activity, including:

  • Questions attempted
  • Questions answered correctly or incorrectly
  • Unattempted questions
  • Test and practice results
  • Marks and scores
  • Accuracy
  • Percentile and rank
  • Time spent on questions or tests
  • Practice history
  • Test history
  • PYQ practice history
  • NTA question practice history
  • Custom practice activity
  • Custom test activity
  • Test-series activity
  • Subject and chapter performance
  • Study streaks and progress
  • Saved questions and tests
  • Leaderboard participation

This information is used to provide personalized learning, performance analysis, rankings, statistics and other educational features.

3. Device and Technical Information

We may automatically collect limited technical information necessary to operate, secure and improve our services, such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Browser information
  • Application version
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from IP address where necessary
  • Log information
  • Error and diagnostic information
  • Authentication and session information

We use this information for security, troubleshooting, analytics and service improvement.

4. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Provide Google Sign-In and email-based authentication.
  • Provide NEET and JEE educational content.
  • Deliver practice questions, tests, PYQs and test series.
  • Calculate scores, marks, accuracy, percentile and rankings.
  • Maintain your practice and test history.
  • Provide performance analytics and recommendations.
  • Display leaderboards where applicable.
  • Save questions, tests and user preferences.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized access.
  • Maintain platform security.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Improve our educational products and services.
  • Communicate important service-related information.
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations.

5. How We Use Google User Data

If you use Google Sign-In, Google user information received by Cosmyra is used only for purposes related to providing and operating the requested authentication and account features.

Specifically, basic Google profile information may be used to:

  • Authenticate your identity.
  • Create your Cosmyra account.
  • Sign you into an existing Cosmyra account.
  • Display your name and profile picture within your account where applicable.
  • Associate your Google authentication with your Cosmyra profile.
  • Maintain your account and provide educational services.

We do not sell Google user data.

We do not use Google user data for advertising or sell it to advertising networks or data brokers.

We do not use Google user data for creditworthiness, lending or similar purposes.

We only request Google permissions that are necessary for the functionality we provide.

Our handling of Google user data follows the applicable Google API Services User Data Policy and Limited Use requirements. Google requires developers to clearly disclose how Google user data is accessed, used, stored and shared.
